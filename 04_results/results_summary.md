## WT versus Mutant Protein Comparison

The wild-type GALT protein contains 379 amino acids. The documented mutation c.563A>G changes glutamine at position 188 to arginine (p.Gln188Arg; Q188R), while the artificial mutation c.564G>A changes arginine at the same position to lysine (p.Arg188Lys; R188K). Both mutations are missense substitutions that preserve the reading frame and protein length.
